1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

In Short: We collect information you provide, information generated when you use CashGraph, limited information from service providers, and limited technical information needed to operate and improve the Services.

Personal information you provide to us

The information we collect depends on the features you use and the choices you make.

Account and profile data. When you create or use an account, we may process your email address, Firebase user identifier, display name, profile photo URL, and authentication-provider identifier.

Financial records you enter. CashGraph stores information that you voluntarily enter, including transaction amount, description, date, transaction type (income or expense), category, custom category name, recurrence, account type, and related timestamps. CashGraph does not automatically access or import your bank, card, or merchant-system records.

Planning and settings data. We may process budgets, recurring transaction schedules, currency code and symbol, time-zone offset, onboarding state, reminder preferences, and other settings needed to provide your selected experience.

Smart Entry content. When you use Smart Entry, we process the text you type or the speech transcript shown in the input field, together with limited context needed to interpret the entry, such as the reference date, time-zone offset, and supported category list.

AI Habit Coach data. When you request Habit Coach insights, we process a limited snapshot derived from recent CashGraph records. This can include verified totals, income and expense counts, active-day counts, category totals and frequencies, rhythm labels, existing budget categories, a possible budget candidate, and detected recurring-charge names and amounts. CashGraph is designed not to send raw transaction notes to Habit Coach, except that a detected recurring-charge name may be derived from a description you entered.

Support communications. If you contact us, we process the information included in your message and your contact details so we can respond.

Speech recognition and microphone access

If you choose to use voice input, CashGraph requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions. Audio is used to produce a live transcript through the device's speech-recognition services. CashGraph does not store the microphone recording in its own database or send the audio recording to the CashGraph backend. If you submit the resulting transcript through Smart Entry, the transcript is processed as Smart Entry content described above. The device platform's handling of speech recognition is also governed by the platform provider's terms and privacy practices.

Payment and subscription data

Payments are processed by Apple through App Store In-App Purchases. CashGraph does not receive or store full card details. Apple and RevenueCat provide subscription and purchase information needed to validate receipts, prevent fraud, restore purchases, determine entitlement to premium features, and provide subscription analytics. This may include product identifier, purchase and expiration status, transaction and receipt information, subscription history, and a RevenueCat customer identifier linked to your CashGraph user identifier.

You can review the providers' notices at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

Data collected automatically

Usage analytics. In production builds, and according to your in-app analytics setting, Firebase Analytics may collect limited product-interaction information such as screens viewed, onboarding progress, feature requests and outcomes, navigation choices, subscription-funnel steps, session information, app instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, language, and an approximate country derived from an IP address. We do not place transaction descriptions, amounts, categories, dates, balances, budgets, Smart Entry text or speech transcripts, AI prompts or outputs, email addresses, names, or account identifiers in our custom analytics events.

Crash, performance, and diagnostic data. Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring may process crash reports, stack traces, relevant app state, app version, device model, operating-system version, installation identifiers, startup and network-performance measurements, CPU or memory information, IP address, and other technical details needed to diagnose reliability and performance. These services operate in production builds and are separate from the optional usage-analytics setting.

Backend security and operation data. Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions may process authentication records, request metadata, function name, IP address, timestamps, usage counts, error information, and other operational data needed to authenticate requests, enforce limits, prevent abuse, and run the Services.

Approximate location. CashGraph does not request precise device-location permission. Google services may infer a coarse location, such as country or region, from an IP address for analytics, security, and service operation.

Information from third parties

We may receive:

  • name, email address, profile photo, and a provider identifier from Google Sign-In when you choose that login method;
  • authentication and service metadata from Firebase;
  • purchase, receipt, entitlement, subscription, and restoration information from Apple and RevenueCat; and
  • generated results and service metadata from Google Gemini API when you use an AI feature.

Sensitive information

We do not ask for or intentionally collect special-category information such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, health data, genetic data, or biometric identifiers. Do not enter such information into transaction descriptions, Smart Entry, or support messages. CashGraph also does not request bank-login credentials, card PINs, or full payment-card numbers.

Google API data

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, where applicable: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy.

5. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?

In Short: Yes. CashGraph uses Google's Gemini API for Smart Entry and a limited part of AI Habit Coach. AI is activated only when you initiate the relevant feature.

Smart Entry

When you press send in Smart Entry, CashGraph sends Google Gemini API:

  • the text you typed or the speech transcript displayed in the Smart Entry field;
  • the current reference date and time-zone offset;
  • supported CashGraph category labels; and
  • instructions needed to return proposed transaction fields.

Gemini returns structured suggestions such as amount, description, category, transaction type, date, recurrence, and account type. You can review the result before adding it to CashGraph.

AI Habit Coach

When you activate AI Habit Coach, CashGraph calculates verified financial facts from a limited recent analysis window before contacting Gemini. Gemini may receive totals and counts, active-day counts, category totals and frequency, qualitative rhythm labels, budget context, and detected recurring-charge names and amounts. CashGraph does not send the full transaction list or raw transaction notes to the Habit Coach narrative request, although a detected recurring-charge name can be based on a description you entered.

The numeric parts of the report are generated from CashGraph's verified calculations. Gemini is used for a limited qualitative observation and may still produce an inaccurate or unexpected result.

AI provider processing

CashGraph uses Gemini API as a paid service through a Google Cloud project with active billing. Under Google's current terms for paid Gemini API services, Google states that it does not use prompts or responses to improve its products. Google may log prompts and responses for a limited period for abuse prevention, safety, security, and legal or regulatory requirements, and it processes operational details such as token counts, service status, errors, authentication data, performance metrics, identifiers, and IP addresses.

CashGraph does not use your AI content to train its own general-purpose AI model. We may cache an AI Habit Coach response locally on your device and in your protected Firebase account data so the same result can be displayed without an unnecessary repeat request. The cache is replaced or becomes inapplicable when the relevant data changes and is deleted through the applicable data or account deletion flow.

Do not submit bank credentials, full card numbers, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive or confidential information through Smart Entry or transaction descriptions.

In general:

  • account and profile data is retained while your account remains active;
  • transactions, budgets, recurring schedules, settings, and associated AI cache data are retained until you delete them, use Delete All Data, or delete your account;

When there is no ongoing legitimate need to process information, we will delete or anonymize it, or securely isolate it until deletion is possible, including where deletion from backups or provider systems takes additional time.

CashGraph uses Firebase Analytics to understand broad feature usage and product funnels in eligible production builds.
